THE CASE STUDY

A thoughtful solution
to a real-world need.

A more human way to find your workspace. This concept case study shows how strategy, design, and engineering can work together to create a clearer experience.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

Verdant wanted to make flexible workspaces feel less transactional. Its old website made it hard for visitors to compare locations, understand amenities, and imagine their next workday.

02 / THE APPROACH

Our approach

We created a warm digital identity and responsive web experience, pairing editorial photography with a simpler discovery flow and clearer location details.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Illustrative outcome

A more coherent brand experience, easier browsing on mobile, and a smoother path from discovery to inquiry.
